Help Center
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
How do I add a new station?
Go to Select Station from the bottom navigation, then tap "+ Add Station" and fill in the station name, location, nozzles and storage capacity.
How is revenue calculated?
Revenue = Litres Sold × Price per Litre, for each fuel grade. This is computed automatically on the Dashboard and Sales report screens - you never need to enter it manually.
Can I switch between stations?
Yes - tap the station name at the top of the Dashboard, or use Select Station from the navigation, to switch which station's data you're viewing.
What does the low-stock alert mean?
It means a tank's theoretical stock has dropped below its reorder threshold. Tap the alert on the Dashboard to go to Tanks and place a reorder.
